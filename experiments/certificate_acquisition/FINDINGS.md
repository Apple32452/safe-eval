# Certificate-guided acquisition: held-out screen

**26 October 2026. Prespecified matched-efficiency gate: FAILED.** This
negative result is retained. The candidate does not establish a scalable
cost advantage over the same-inference row-uniform control or the earlier
peer-cell policy. There is no new comparative cost theorem.

The frozen [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) was committed before the full target run.
The method scores each eligible individual cell by its predicted one-step
log-evidence contribution computed under the row-uniform proposal. It then
uses that score for actual acquisition and recalibrates each e-factor using
the actual acquisition probabilities. The earlier validity argument and
factor implementation are unchanged. A repeat-0 one-hot smoke check was
excluded before the run; primary repetitions were 1000–1059.

The interval for each matched cell-level control contains zero, and the
point estimates are negative. Savings against older paired methods do not
establish a distinct benefit from the new acquisition score, because the
matched controls use the same predictor and calibration and are cheaper.
These intervals are approximate case-sampling summaries and do not validate
anytime error rates.

The reversed-history loss is material: a policy optimized for a misspecified
historical predictor can spend many more labels even while its certificate
remains valid. Zero observed errors is not a proof of coverage; the separate
conditional-null factor checks audit that property.

committed here. No human judgments or WMT target data entered this study.

## Next scientific step

Stop tuning this cell-score family on these cases. The practical shortfall
of matched acquisition policies suggests formulating an explicit condition
under which a cell-level policy saves cost relative to the same-inference
row-uniform policy, then testing that condition on a separate data source.
The small exact separation and the EC² prior-work overlap in the previous
study remain the novelty boundaries. A larger synthetic saving versus Stage 22
alone would not resolve them.

# Stage 25: partial-cell theoretical feasibility

New branch only; no WMT24 access, WMT experiments, previous-stage modification or tuning. Theory and novelty gates take priority over more data.

## Candidate and comparison boundary

Query one system-item cell at a time. Choose the system predictably, then draw uniformly from that system's unobserved items with fresh randomization. The candidate uses a fixed equal hedge of product and mixture finite-pool likelihood wealth. Its allocator first finishes the highest-degree graph vertex (lowest-index tie break), then queries an unresolved endpoint with the largest degree-times-remaining-count. Systems cease to be queried when no unresolved edge needs them. Every label is cached once. Item selection is randomized; system selection responds to prior labels through edge resolution.

This construction is a feasibility witness. Product/mixture evidence and adaptive stream allocation are known. It is not presented as novel merely because applied to cells. A novelty GO requires a separation not already achieved by an applicable existing joint method.

Controls: unchanged Stage 22 paired evidence and pruning; product-PPR joint CS; mixture-PPR joint CS; a PEAK-style finite-pool adaptation using its published scalar factor with the correct next-draw remaining mean (c=0.26), then averaging capitals; and the candidate hedge with round-robin allocation. The partial-cell joint controls share the candidate allocator; round-robin isolates allocation. Each targets the same full-pool requested signs with family error <=0.05, including ties, and has budget mN. No independent-data assumptions across system values.

The PEAK adaptation is labeled as an adaptation, not original author code or the original constant-mean theorem. Product and mixture PPR are known joint likelihood/martingale controls, not a claim to have benchmarked every strongest universal-portfolio construction. Ryu/Wornell's published WoR reduction must pass a literal-formula small-pool check before being used; a failed check is a baseline-admissibility issue, not evidence that our method beats it.

Six predeclared matrix families: extreme reference star (reference all ones, other systems all zero), reversed extreme, strongly correlated gaps, independent gaps, exact ties, antagonistic systems. Star and complete graphs. 20 path seeds per cell. Total 24 fixed cells, at most 480 paths per method. Seed 20261030. Generate and hash all fixtures before executing any method. Stage 22 uses a uniform common item permutation and fixed within-item order [0,1,2]. All partial-cell methods use the same per-system uniform sequences; differing allocation/certificates can produce differing purchased cells, so identical paths are not claimed. Report conclusions at 25/50/75/100% equal unique-label budgets, terminal cost, failures and worst cases.

Predeclared GO: valid proof, exact small-state tests, genuine novelty beyond existing stream/joint martingale methods, at least 5% average savings against Stage 22 AND every tested joint control, and no family exceeding 5% excess cost. Otherwise NO-GO for this candidate, not impossibility of all partial-cell research. Do not tune after observing results.
